Rim glyphs

A visual language taken from the inside of a race wheel. The tire is gone; what's left is the rim face: spokes, windows, the lug ring and the hub. Each pattern is distinct at a glance, so it can stand for an agent, a category or a state. Every glyph is named after a circuit.

The library

24 rim faces in six families. Hover to turn one; click to open it in the builder below.

How a glyph is built

Every glyph is one disc with shapes cut out of it, in the same order a rim is machined. Swapping one step changes the whole character.

Families as categories

The family carries the category; the count and detail carry the individual agent. Two agents in Vuln Management share straight spokes but never the same number.

In the product

As agent marks in the library, in each agent's own color, at the sizes the interface actually uses.
